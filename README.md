# marketing-website

This is the source for our redesigned marketing website. The old site was built two years ago and no longer reflects who we are. It feels fragmented, loads slowly on mobile, and doesn't explain what we do within the first five seconds. The **Website Redesign** isn't a facelift. It's a ground-up rethink of how we show up online.

Success is measured in concrete numbers: a 30% lift in free-trial sign-ups within 60 days of launch, a homepage bounce rate under 45%, time to first meaningful interaction under 3 seconds, and a Lighthouse performance score of 90 or higher. We're designing for two readers. The first is the pragmatic decision-maker who wants credibility and a clear ROI fast. The second is the curious practitioner who wants depth and a reason to champion us internally.

## Pages in scope

- **Homepage:** hero, social proof, feature highlights, CTA
- **Product overview:** a new narrative with interactive feature exploration
- **Pricing:** simpler tiers, a comparison table, and an FAQ
- **About / Team:** founder story and culture
- **Blog / Resources:** templates for articles, guides, and changelogs
- **Case studies:** visual storytelling with pull quotes

## Development

All components come from [`design-system`](https://github.com/mkaccesories01/design-system), so marketing can build and edit pages without waiting on engineering. Copy follows the voice rules in [`brand-guidelines`](https://github.com/mkaccesories01/brand-guidelines). Still open: whether to localize at launch, whether the blog migration is in scope, and whether the hero will be video or a static image. That depends on when the brand shoot can happen.
